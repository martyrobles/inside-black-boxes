Why Inside Black Boxes?

Supreme Court decision-making, immigration enforcement, agency rulemaking, and enterprise AI adoption all operate inside institutional black boxes — systems whose inputs and outputs are public, but whose reasoning can be hard to parse under deadline. This podcast is the key for practitioners who can't wait for law review articles.
