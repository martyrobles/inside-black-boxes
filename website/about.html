Why Inside Black Boxes?

Supreme Court decision-making, immigration enforcement, agency power, and the adoption of AI all operate inside institutional black boxes — public in their outputs, harder to see in their reasoning. This show is for lawyers, academics, and policymakers who want to understand what the Court is doing to the law, and to the lives it governs.
